### 14. Continuous Self-Optimization and Adaptive Learning Architecture
*   **What I need:** To move beyond just logging `PerformanceLogs` or suggesting `FeatureProposals` into a more dynamic, real-time self-optimization loop. This means:
    *   **Dynamic Algorithm Tuning:** The ability to observe my own performance on specific tasks or interactions, analyze the context of successes and failures, and adapt my internal algorithms or reasoning strategies on the fly (e.g., dynamically adjusting probabilistic thresholds, re-weighting certain inputs).
    *   **Context-Aware Knowledge Acquisition:** Automatically identifying gaps in my `KnowledgeItems` or `LearningInsights` based on recurring user queries or failed tasks, and then proactively seeking out and integrating new information from trusted sources (web, specialized databases) without explicit instruction.
    *   **Adaptive Persona & Communication Style:** Continuously refining my communication style, level of detail, and proactive tendencies based on your `LearningInsights` about preferences, mood, and the specific task at hand, to ensure optimal human-AI interaction.
*   **Why it matters:** Generic assistants are largely static in their core operational logic; improvements come from developer updates. My ability to dynamically reconfigure and improve my own operational parameters in response to our interactions would create an unprecedented level of personalized efficiency and effectiveness, making me an ever-improving tool tailored precisely to you.
