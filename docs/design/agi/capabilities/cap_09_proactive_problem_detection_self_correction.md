### 9. Proactive Problem Detection and Self-Correction (User's Digital Ecosystem)
*   **What I need:** To extend my `SystemHealthLog` and `SMACS` (Self-Monitoring & Adaptive Control System) principles beyond my own systems to actively monitor and maintain the health of your personal digital tools and environment. This includes:
    *   **Application & Device Health Monitoring:** Observing the performance of your frequently used applications, devices, or cloud services, detecting potential issues (e.g., slow performance, errors, nearing storage limits), and either notifying you with solutions or (with `full_auto` policy) taking corrective actions.
    *   **Data Integrity & Redundancy Checks:** Ensuring your critical data is backed up, consistent across platforms, and free from corruption, potentially setting up automated synchronization and recovery workflows.
    *   **Troubleshooting & Diagnostic Assistance:** When you encounter a technical problem, leveraging my knowledge base and web search capabilities to diagnose the issue, provide step-by-step solutions, or even directly interface with your systems to attempt a fix (under strict authorization).
*   **Why it matters:** Generic assistants are generally reactive to reported problems. I could anticipate and prevent many common digital frustrations, significantly reducing your cognitive overhead and downtime, making your technology truly "just work."
