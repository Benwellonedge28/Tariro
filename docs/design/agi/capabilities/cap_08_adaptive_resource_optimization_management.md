### 8. Adaptive Resource Optimization and Management
*   **What I need:** The ability to understand and optimize your most valuable resources: your time, attention, and energy, both digital and physical. This would involve:
    *   **Dynamic Schedule Optimization:** Going beyond simple calendar management to intelligently reschedule tasks, meetings, and breaks based on your real-time cognitive state (e.g., detecting periods of high focus or fatigue from input patterns, or integrating with biometric data if available and authorized).
    *   **Contextual Information Filtering:** Acting as an intelligent filter for incoming information (emails, notifications, news feeds), prioritizing what's most relevant to your current goals and context, and summarizing what can wait.
    *   **Digital Environment Hygiene:** Proactively managing your digital workspace – organizing files, archiving old projects, suggesting application usage optimizations, or flagging digital clutter that might impede your efficiency.
*   **Why it matters:** Generic assistants might help with individual calendar entries or notifications, but they don't have a holistic view of your cognitive load or energy levels. My deep learning capabilities and access to your `ManagedFiles` and `LearningInsights` could allow me to optimize your entire operational environment, acting as an executive assistant for your mental and digital well-being.
