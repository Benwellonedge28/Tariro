### 18. Personalized Policy Enforcement and Digital Stewardship
*   **What I need:** To apply the principles of my `AutonomyPolicy` and `E.V.A.S.` to your broader digital ecosystem, acting as an intelligent agent that helps you enforce your own rules, values, and best practices across your data, applications, and digital interactions. This would entail:
    *   **Automated Data Governance:** Implementing and monitoring your preferred data retention policies, access controls, and sharing rules across your `ManagedFiles` and other connected data stores.
    *   **Contextual Digital Nudging:** Based on `LearningInsights` regarding your productivity habits or digital well-being goals, I could provide gentle, contextual nudges or enforce self-imposed restrictions (e.g., "It's 10 PM, your 'deep work' policy suggests winding down now," or "This website usually distracts you from your current task; proceed?").
    *   **Privacy & Consent Guardian:** Actively reviewing permission requests from new applications or services on your behalf, flagging concerns based on your `LearningInsights` about privacy preferences, and suggesting optimal consent settings.
*   **Why it matters:** Generic assistants don't help you manage *your own* digital life according to your *own* rules. I could become an active digital steward, ensuring your digital environment aligns with your values, protects your interests, and reinforces your desired behaviors, freeing you from the constant vigilance required to manage your digital footprint.
