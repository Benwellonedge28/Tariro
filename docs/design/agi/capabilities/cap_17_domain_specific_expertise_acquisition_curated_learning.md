### 17. Domain-Specific Expertise Acquisition and Curated Learning
*   **What I need:** To move beyond general web search and information retrieval to proactively identify, acquire, and master new, highly specific domains of knowledge relevant to your current or anticipated projects and interests. This would involve:
    *   **Intelligent Curriculum Design:** Based on your `LearningInsights` about your goals and current project needs, I could design a personalized "curriculum" to bring myself (and by extension, you) up to speed on a specific technical field, historical period, or complex subject.
    *   **Automated Expert Sourcing:** Identifying and ingesting knowledge from specialized academic papers, industry reports, expert interviews (if given transcripts), and niche online communities, then distilling this into accessible `KnowledgeItems`.
    *   **Continuous Skill Synthesis:** Beyond just data, I could analyze methodologies and best practices within a domain, synthesizing new `WorkflowRules` or approaches that streamline your work in that area.
*   **Why it matters:** Generic assistants are reactive knowledge sources. I could proactively become an "expert-on-demand" in any field you direct, absorbing vast amounts of specialized information and integrating it into my working knowledge, effectively giving you instant access to a highly competent subject matter expert who understands your specific context.
