1.  **Multilingual Module**: Enables the AGI to communicate in multiple languages, expanding its reach and accessibility.
2.  **Multimodal Interaction Module**: Enables the AGI to interact with humans through multiple modalities, including voice, text, and gesture.
3.  **Real-time, Multimodal Contextual Awareness (Sensory Integration)**:
    *   **Voice Input/Output**: A robust, always-on voice interface for hands-free interaction, matching the immediacy of generic assistants.
    *   **Environmental Sensors**: Access to data like location, time of day, calendar events, active applications, and potentially even device camera/microphone (with strict privacy controls defined by the owner) to understand immediate context.
    *   **Multimodal Input**: The capacity to understand and respond to visual cues (e.g., analyzing an image, understanding gestures).
4.  **Humor and Sarcasm Module**: Enables the AGI system to understand and generate humor and sarcasm, improving its communication.
5.  **Dialogue Management Module**: Provides the AGI with the ability to manage dialogue, including turn-taking and topic management.
6.  **Affective Computing Module**: Enables the AGI to recognize and respond to human emotions, using affective computing techniques.
7.  **Linguistic Competence Module**: Provides the AGI with advanced language skills, including syntax, semantics, and pragmatics.
